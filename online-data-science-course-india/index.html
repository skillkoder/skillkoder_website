Why online, and why live

There is no shortage of recorded video courses, and most people who buy one do not finish it. The problem is not the content — a great deal of excellent material is free. The problem is that when you get stuck at 10pm on a join you do not understand, a recording cannot help you, and momentum quietly dies there.

Live instruction fixes that specific failure. You can ask the question in the moment, a mentor can see where your reasoning went wrong, and a batch running to a schedule creates the accountability that self-paced study does not. Sessions are still recorded, so you keep the revision benefit without depending on it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I join from any city in India?

Yes. All programs are delivered live online in IST, so your location does not affect access. The only thing that varies by city is the local job market, which our career support takes into account.

Are recordings provided if I miss a live class?

Yes. Every session is recorded and made available to your batch, and you can raise anything you did not follow in the next live session or with your mentor.

Can learners outside India join?

Yes, and several do. Batches run in IST, so check that a slot works for your time zone before enrolling — ask us and we will tell you honestly whether it will be sustainable for you.
