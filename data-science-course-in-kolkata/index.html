The honest position first

There are fewer dedicated data science roles in Kolkata than there are analytics roles, and considerably fewer than in Bengaluru, Hyderabad or Pune. The city's technology employment is weighted toward IT services and business process operations around Salt Lake Sector V and New Town, with a significant banking and financial services back-office presence.

We are telling you this before describing the course because it should affect how you plan, and you will discover it anyway.

What follows from that

  • Remote roles deserve serious consideration. The skills in this course qualify you for them regardless of where you sit, and remote hiring has become a practical route out of a thin local market.
  • Analytics-first may be the faster path. Local analytics and MIS demand is real, and moving into modelling work from inside a company is often easier than being hired directly into it here. See the Data Analytics course in Kolkata.
  • BFSI back-office work generates genuine modelling demand — risk, fraud and reconciliation problems that are more interesting than they sound.
  • Cost of living is markedly lower than the southern hubs, which changes what a given salary is actually worth.

Targeting remote data science roles

Remote roles are more competitive per opening and favour candidates who visibly work well without supervision. Two things matter disproportionately: a portfolio that is genuinely self-directed rather than course-assigned, and written communication, because most of the job happens in writing when the team is distributed.

Both are worked on during career support — see what placement support includes.

What the course covers

  • Python, pandas and NumPy — with real time on the data preparation that dominates the actual job.
  • SQL — you still have to extract data before you can model it, and it appears in data science interviews regularly.
  • scikit-learn and the modelling workflow — pipelines, cross-validation, feature engineering and hyperparameter search.
  • Honest evaluation — metric choice, baselines, and catching data leakage before an interviewer does.
  • TensorFlow and PyTorch — network fundamentals and fine-tuning pretrained models.

What you build

A complete modelling workflow on a real dataset: cleaning decisions documented, features engineered with reasoning, models compared against a baseline, and a metric chosen and justified. Deliberately not Titanic or Iris — interviewers have seen those thousands of times and they signal nothing.

For a remote job search this matters more, not less. A portfolio is most of what a distributed hiring team can assess.

Frequently asked questions

Are there data science jobs in Kolkata?

There is real demand, concentrated in IT services, business process operations and BFSI functions, but fewer dedicated data science roles than in the southern tech hubs. Remote roles are worth targeting alongside local ones, and the skills transfer directly.

Should I do data analytics first if I am in Kolkata?

For many people here, yes. Local analytics demand is stronger, and moving into modelling work from inside a company faces far less competition than being hired directly into a junior data science role.

Are the data science classes held in Kolkata?

No — every session is live and online, attended from wherever you are. We have no campus in Kolkata and would rather say so plainly than let you assume otherwise.
