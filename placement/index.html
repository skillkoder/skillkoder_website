Placement Assistance, Described Honestly

Resume review, portfolio guidance, mock interviews and job search strategy — running alongside the course rather than tacked on at the end. Instead of an unverifiable placement percentage, this page lists the specific things we do for you and states plainly what sits outside our control.

  • Resume and LinkedIn review against the roles you are targeting
  • Portfolio guidance that turns course projects into interview material
  • Mock interviews with direct feedback on what would have ended the real round
  • Technical interview preparation in SQL, Python, modelling and pipeline design
  • Job search strategy and mentor access while you are interviewing

What placement support includes

Resume and LinkedIn review

A line-by-line review of your CV against the specific roles you are applying for — rewriting project descriptions so a recruiter can see the tools and the outcome in the first five seconds, which is roughly how long yours gets.

Portfolio guidance

Your course projects turned into something presentable: a clean repository, a readable summary of the problem and result, and a version of the story you can tell out loud in an interview without notes.

Mock interviews

Practice rounds covering SQL and Python problems, case-style business questions, and the project deep-dive where most candidates come unstuck. You get direct feedback on what would have ended the real interview.

Technical interview prep

Structured preparation for the rounds that actually filter candidates — window functions and joins in SQL, model evaluation reasoning in Data Science, pipeline design in Data Engineering.

Job search strategy

Where to apply, how to prioritise, and how to approach referrals. Most people lose months applying broadly through job portals; a targeted approach with a clear positioning statement works considerably better.

Mentor access during the search

Your mentor stays reachable while you are interviewing, so you can bring back real questions from real rounds instead of guessing at what went wrong.

Frequently asked questions

Is a job guaranteed after completing the course?

No, and you should be cautious of any training provider that promises one. What we provide is placement assistance: resume and portfolio work, mock interviews, technical preparation and job search guidance. Getting hired also depends on your own effort, the roles you target and market conditions at the time.

When does placement support start?

Career support runs alongside the technical curriculum rather than being bolted on at the end. Resume and portfolio work begins once you have your first substantial project, so you are not starting from a blank page in the final week.

How long does placement support last after the course ends?

Support continues past the last class while you are actively interviewing. Speak to us about the current terms for the batch you are joining so you have that in writing before you enrol.

Do you help with roles outside India?

The preparation transfers — the tools and interview formats are broadly the same. Our direct market knowledge and network are strongest in India, so treat guidance on other markets as general rather than specific.

What if I am switching careers from a non-technical background?

Career switchers are a large share of our learners. The extra work is usually positioning rather than technical: framing your existing domain experience as an advantage instead of a gap. That framing is something we work on with you directly.
