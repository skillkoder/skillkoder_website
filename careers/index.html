Data & AI Career Guides

Four roles, described as they actually are rather than as the job titles suggest. Start from the work you want to be doing and pick the route backwards from there.

Most people choose a course first and work out the destination afterwards, which is the wrong way round and costs a lot of wasted effort. These guides describe what each role involves on an ordinary working day, what employers genuinely screen for, and the realistic route in — including where each path is harder than it is usually presented.

If you are not sure where to start

Read how to become a data analyst first, even if you suspect it is not where you want to end up. It is the most common entry point, the shortest path from a non-technical background, and the skills it builds — SQL, data quality judgement and stakeholder communication — are assumed by all three of the others.

A great many people reach data science or engineering by starting as an analyst and moving across internally, which faces far less competition than applying externally into a senior role you are not yet qualified for.

Why there are no salary figures on these pages Because we do not have a citable source we are willing to stand behind, and an invented range on a page selling training is a false claim to someone deciding how to spend course fees. Check current listings on the job boards you would actually apply through, for your city and experience level — that is better data than any article's national average.
